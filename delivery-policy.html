Delivery Policy

Last updated:

The Ultimate Video Editing Bundle (2026) Pro Toolkit is a digital product. Nothing is shipped physically, and there are no shipping or delivery charges.

1. How delivery works

  • After your payment is successful, you get instant direct download access to the Bundle.
  • The download access is provided by our checkout provider (SuperProfile), on the page shown after payment and/or by email to the email address you entered at checkout.
  • Delivery is usually immediate. In rare cases (payment confirmation delays or email delays) it may take a little longer.

2. If you did not receive access

  • Check your email inbox, including the Spam/Junk and Promotions folders.
  • Make sure you entered the correct email address at checkout.
  • If you still cannot find it, contact us with your order ID or payment reference and the email/phone used at checkout. We will help you get access.

3. Downloading the files

The files are large. Please use a stable internet connection and download them on a Windows computer with enough free storage space. We recommend keeping a backup copy of the downloaded files.

4. Access period

You get lifetime access to the files you download.
