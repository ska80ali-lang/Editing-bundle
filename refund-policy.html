Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

The Ultimate Video Editing Bundle (2026) Pro Toolkit is a digital product. You get download access immediately after payment, and downloaded files cannot be "returned". For this reason, all sales are final, except in the cases listed below.

1. Please check before you buy

  • This bundle is for Windows, not Mac.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro must be installed separately on your computer. It is not included.

These requirements are clearly shown on the product page. We cannot offer a refund because you use a Mac, do not have Adobe Premiere Pro, or changed your mind after purchase.

2. When you are eligible for a refund

  • No access received: your payment was successful but you did not receive download access, and we are unable to give you access within 48 hours of you contacting us.
  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same order. The extra payment will be refunded.
  • Faulty files: the files are corrupted or will not download, and we are unable to provide a working replacement within 7 days of you contacting us.

3. How to request a refund

Email us within 7 days of purchase with your order ID, the email/phone used at checkout, and a short description of the problem (screenshots help). Use the contact details at the bottom of this page.

4. Refund processing

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method. Once approved, the refund is usually initiated within 5–7 business days. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

5. Cancellation

Because access is delivered instantly after payment, an order cannot be cancelled once payment is complete. If your payment failed but money was debited, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or the payment provider. If it is not, contact us with the transaction details and we will help.
